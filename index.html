About Me

Thinking like a PM.
Building like a founder.

I don't just make things look good — I understand the business logic behind them.

I'm Thameem Apsara Banu, a Product Manager, Web Designer, and AI Automation specialist based in Chennai, India.

I spent 3 years at Hexnode (Mitsogo) — a B2B SaaS enterprise platform — leading product work across IAM, UEM, compliance, and device operations for fleets of 45,000+ devices globally. I didn't just write specs. I did the full research: competitor deep-dives, market analysis, user flow mapping, and interactive prototype-building — then handed developer-ready deliverables to engineering. That process produced 7 enterprise features: a full Identity Provider, the industry's first bidirectional ITSM–UEM integration, deployment ring orchestration, multi-device remote console, enterprise compliance rules, premium analytics, and session transfer.

Now I run For Your Spark, where I bring that same end-to-end product discipline to clients. Research, flow design, interactive prototypes, developer handoff — or full AI-assisted execution when speed matters. Most freelancers hand you a site. I ask why it needs to exist and what it needs to do.
